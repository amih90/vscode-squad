# Meet Your Team

The **Roster** sidebar shows all agents organized by role:

- **Coordinator** — the lead who assigns tasks and reviews work
- **Members** — specialized agents (Backend, Frontend, Security, etc.)
- **Coding Agent** — @copilot or other AI coding assistants

Click any agent to see their detail panel with charter, status, and history.

Right-click agents for quick actions: edit role, view charter, check history.
